The originals are 3D-print-oriented STL files (~45,000-79,000 triangles
each - far more detail than a real-time web scene needs for a piece that's
often small on screen and may appear dozens of times on one board). Each
was decimated with THREE.SimplifyModifier down to a size appropriate for
this project (700-18,000 triangles depending on how much detail the shape
needs to stay recognizable - the knight's head loses its silhouette much
faster than the pawn's simple profile does) and re-exported as GLB.

Triort has no equivalent in real chess - it's this project's own piece -
so there's no source model for it; chess/graphics.js builds its shape
procedurally to sit stylistically alongside these six.
